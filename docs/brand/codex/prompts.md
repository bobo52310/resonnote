## candidate-1 (refine current concept)

```text
Use your image generation tool to create ONE icon image. The attached reference is the current ResonNote icon (a Chrome extension that lets you save the exact second of a TV show or video that moved you: screenshot, timestamp, subtitle line, and your note). Refine and polish this exact concept, keep the same geometry and composition: a dark rounded-square tile (rounded corners about 22 percent of the width), a thick amber (#ffb547) corner bracket in the top-left and another in the bottom-right (they read as both camera viewfinder corners and Chinese quotation marks), and a solid brand-red (#f2483a) dot in the exact center. Make it more refined and premium: subtle vertical gradient on the tile (warm near-black #2a241c at top to #14110d at bottom), a soft inner highlight rim along the tile edge, gentle amber gradient on the brackets (slightly lighter amber at the top edge, richer amber at the bottom) with rounded caps, and a tiny soft glow plus a small specular highlight on the red dot so it feels lit. Keep it flat enough for 16px. Hard requirements: this is an app / Chrome-extension icon. Centered, single subject, the icon shape fills about 80 percent of the 1024x1024 canvas. Flat to semi-flat vector-like look that stays recognizable when shrunk to 16px: bold chunky shapes only, no thin lines, no tiny details, no small ornaments. Absolutely NO text, NO letters, NO numbers, NO logos of any streaming platform (no Netflix N, no YouTube play button, no Disney castle). Not a photograph, not photorealistic, no 3D render. Do not use Netflix red #e50914. Palette: cinema black #0b0a09 / #141210 / #1f1c18, amber #ffb547, brand red #f2483a, warm off-white #f5f2ef only as a tiny accent. Output a single square 1024x1024 PNG. Outside the icon shape the background must be one flat solid pure-white #ffffff (so it can be keyed out) with no shadow or glow bleeding outside the shape.
Save the final PNG by copying it to OUT/raw/candidate-1.png, then reply with one short line.
```

```text

Hard requirements: a dark, cinematic, moody BACKGROUND image used behind overlaid text and a logo, wide landscape format, 3:2 or 16:9 (for example 1536x1024). Strict palette: cinema black #0b0a09 and #141210 as the base, warm amber #ffb547 as the main light colour, and brand red #f2483a only as one or two tiny accents (for example a single small red indicator light). No other hues, no teal, no blue, no purple. ABSOLUTELY NO text, letters, numbers, subtitles, captions or watermarks anywhere. NO logos, NO platform UI, NO real show or movie footage, NO recognisable people, NO faces. Not a photograph of a real show. Soft, painterly or lightly photographic cinematic depth of field is fine, but keep it clean: no noise, no scratches, no strange artifacts, no sharp tiny details. The attached image is the app icon: use it only as a style and colour reference (dark warm black, amber, one red dot); do NOT paste or redraw the icon in the scene.
